# Stakeholder Communication Template

This template helps program managers define how stakeholders are informed and engaged throughout a technical program.

## Stakeholder Communication Plan

| Stakeholder | Role | Communication Type | Frequency | Owner | Notes |
|------------|------|-------------------|----------|------|------|
| Executive Sponsor | Strategic oversight | Executive update | Monthly | Program Lead | Focus on risks, milestones, and decisions |
| Engineering Leads | Delivery teams | Coordination meeting | Weekly | TPM | Dependency and milestone review |

Communication plans should clearly identify who owns each communication channel and how frequently updates occur. If stakeholders are unclear about how or when they will receive program updates, those gaps should remain visible so the program team can establish appropriate communication rhythms.
