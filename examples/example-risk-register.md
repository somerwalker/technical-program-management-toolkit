# Example Risk Register

This example demonstrates how risks might be documented and tracked in a technical program.

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation | Owner |
|--------|-----------------|--------|-----------|-----------|------|
| R1 | Vendor hardware delivery delay | High | Medium | Weekly vendor checkpoint | Infrastructure Lead |
| R2 | Engineering team resource constraints | Medium | High | Adjust workstream priorities | Program Lead |
