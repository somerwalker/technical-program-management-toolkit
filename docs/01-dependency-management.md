# Dependency Management

This document outlines practical approaches for identifying, tracking, and coordinating dependencies across teams in complex technical programs.

Technical programs often require coordination across multiple teams, services, and systems. Dependency management ensures that workstreams remain aligned and that potential blockers are surfaced early.

## Types of Dependencies

Dependencies typically fall into several categories.

### Technical Dependencies
When one system or component must be completed before another can proceed.

### Resource Dependencies
When teams share engineers, infrastructure, or tools.

### External Dependencies
When delivery relies on vendors, partners, or external organizations.

### Decision Dependencies
When execution depends on leadership approvals or architectural decisions.

## Effective Dependency Tracking

Strong programs track dependencies with:

- clear ownership  
- expected delivery dates  
- impact assessment  
- mitigation strategies  
- visibility across teams  

## Best Practices

Effective dependency management includes:

- documenting dependencies early during planning  
- maintaining a shared dependency tracker  
- reviewing dependencies during coordination meetings  
- escalating delays quickly when milestones are affected  

Dependency visibility significantly improves program predictability.
