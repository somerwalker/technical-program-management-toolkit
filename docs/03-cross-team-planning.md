# Cross-Team Planning

This document explains how large technical programs coordinate planning across multiple teams to align milestones, dependencies, and delivery schedules.

Large technical programs often require collaboration across multiple engineering teams.

## Planning Objectives

Cross-team planning helps organizations:

- coordinate timelines across teams  
- identify shared milestones  
- clarify ownership of deliverables  
- anticipate dependencies between workstreams  

## Planning Artifacts

Common artifacts used during planning include:

- program charters  
- delivery roadmaps  
- milestone plans  
- dependency maps  

## Planning Process

Typical planning activities include:

1. Define program objectives and scope  
2. Identify participating teams  
3. Map workstreams and dependencies  
4. Align milestone timelines  
5. Confirm ownership and accountability  

Strong planning reduces surprises during execution.
